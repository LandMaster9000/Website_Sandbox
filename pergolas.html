Custom Pergola Projects in Charleston, SC

A pergola is an open structure. Rafters overhead instead of a solid roof, so you get filtered shade without closing the space in. It gives an area definition and a ceiling line while keeping the sky, which is why it works well over a patio, a seating area, a walkway, or along the side of a house where a full roof would feel heavy. We design and build custom pergolas across Charleston, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, James Island and Daniel Island, freestanding in the yard or attached to the house over an existing patio or deck.

Pergola Builds for Lowcountry Homes

The question we get most is pergola or pavilion. A pergola filters light and keeps the space open. A pavilion has a solid roof and keeps the rain off. If the goal is a patio you can still sit under in a downpour, that is a pavilion. If the goal is shade, structure, and somewhere to train vines without losing the openness, that is a pergola. Attached or freestanding shapes the design as much as the size does. An attached pergola has to work with the roofline, the fascia and the door it extends from. A freestanding one is placed to suit the yard and where the sun actually falls, and we work that out on site.
