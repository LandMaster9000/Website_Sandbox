Irrigation and Sprinkler System Installation in Charleston, SC

A thriving landscape starts with consistent, efficient water management. At Cramers Landscaping, we specialize in professional irrigation and sprinkler system installation for residential and commercial properties throughout Charleston and the surrounding Lowcountry. Whether you need a brand-new sprinkler system, drip irrigation for flower beds, or a custom water management plan, our team delivers systems that protect your investment and reduce waste.

From smart controllers to zone-specific design, we install irrigation systems tailored to your property's layout, plant material, soil conditions, and long-term needs.

We also service and repair existing systems. If heads are misting or spraying the driveway, a zone has stopped running, a valve is leaking, or the controller is watering at the wrong times, we will find the fault and repair it rather than replace what is still working.

Why proper Irrigation Matters

Charleston’s climate is uniquely challenging for outdoor landscapes. Hot, humid summers, periodic droughts, heavy rainfall, and sandy or clay-heavy soils can all lead to plant stress, erosion, or overwatering if not properly managed. That’s where our irrigation experts come in. A well-designed irrigation system:
  • Delivers water efficiently to the root zone
  • Reduces water waste and utility costs
  • Prevents runoff and erosion
  • Supports healthy, consistent plant growth
  • Protects high-value softscapes during dry periods
We install irrigation systems that not only keep your lawn and garden healthy but also save you time and money.

What We Install

Every irrigation system we install is designed to match the scale, usage, and vegetation needs of your landscape. Our systems include:
Zoned Sprinkler Systems

We divide your property into zones based on sun exposure, plant types, and soil conditions. Each zone is watered independently to avoid over- or under-watering.

Drip Irrigation

Perfect for flower beds, vegetable gardens, trees, and shrubs, drip irrigation systems provide slow, targeted water directly to the soil—conserving water and minimizing evaporation.

Smart Controllers

We install smart irrigation timers and controllers that can be managed from your smartphone. These systems monitor weather patterns, adjust watering schedules, and reduce overwatering.

Rain Sensors

As an optional add-on, we include rain shut-off sensors to automatically pause watering during and after rainfall, helping to conserve water and avoid root rot.

Backflow Prevention

All of our systems include properly installed backflow preventers to protect the public water supply, as required by local code.

Common Charleston Irrigation Challenges We Solve

Sandy Soils

Charleston’s coastal soil composition often causes fast drainage, which can leave plant roots dry. Our systems are designed to deliver deeper, more frequent watering to match local soil conditions.

Sloped Lawns

Water tends to pool or run off on sloped properties. We use low-pressure heads and smart zoning to prevent oversaturation and erosion.

High Utility Costs

We design systems that are efficient—not just effective. Smart controllers, drip emitters, and zoned schedules keep your bills manageable.

Our Installation Process

We approach every landscape installation project with a clear, proven process:

Site Evaluation

We walk your property and evaluate plant types, sun exposure, slopes, soil conditions, and water access points.

Design Plan

We design a custom irrigation layout, determine zones, recommend drip vs. spray applications, and ensure coverage overlaps correctly for uniform watering.

Trenching and Pipe Installation

We carefully trench your property, minimizing turf disturbance, and install high-quality piping, valves, and heads according to spec.

Controller Programming

After installation, we program your system based on seasonality, zone type, and local regulations. For smart systems, we walk you through mobile access and management.

Testing and Final Walkthrough

We test all zones, fine-tune spray patterns, and ensure your system is operating efficiently and leak-free. You’ll receive clear care instructions and optional maintenance schedules.
