Frequently Asked Questions

How long does concrete take to cure in Charleston’s climate?

Concrete typically cures within 28 days, but humidity and temperature can affect drying time. Our team uses proven curing techniques and sealers to ensure strong, even results in Charleston’s weather.

What’s the difference between stamped and regular concrete?

Stamped concrete Charleston SC replicates the look of stone, pavers, or wood using custom molds and coloring. It’s decorative yet durable, offering aesthetic appeal without sacrificing strength.

How do I maintain my concrete surfaces?

Clean your concrete periodically with mild detergent and water, and reseal every few years to protect against stains and moisture. We also provide professional sealing services to extend your concrete’s lifespan.

Can you resurface or pour new concrete over old?

Yes, as long as the existing surface is stable. We can pour overlays or resurface old patios, driveways, and concrete pool decks to restore appearance and performance without complete demolition.

How do I prevent my driveway or patio from cracking?

Proper base prep and control joints are essential. As an experienced Charleston concrete company, we engineer every project to handle soil movement, drainage, and temperature shifts that cause cracking.
