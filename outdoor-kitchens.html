Outdoor Kitchens Designed for Lowcountry Living

Cramers Landscaping brings your backyard to life with custom outdoor kitchens designed for entertaining, cooking, and relaxing in the Charleston sun. As one of the Lowcountry’s most trusted landscaping and outdoor living specialists, we create functional, elegant spaces that seamlessly integrate with your landscape and architecture—blending craftsmanship, design, and utility in one expertly built feature.

Whether you’re hosting summer cookouts, tailgating for game day, or sharing quiet weeknight dinners al fresco, our outdoor kitchen installations are built to last, to impress, and to elevate your entire outdoor experience.

Why Invest in an Outdoor Kitchen ?

Charleston’s year-round mild weather makes outdoor kitchens more than just a luxury—they’re a smart lifestyle upgrade that adds value, function, and personality to your home. With a professionally installed outdoor kitchen, you get:
  • A True Extension of Your Home: Expand your usable living space outdoors.
  • Increased Property Value: Outdoor kitchens consistently rank high in ROI for home improvements.
  • Ideal for Entertaining: Keep guests outside while you cook and enjoy the atmosphere together.
  • Better Ventilation & Less Indoor Mess: No lingering smoke or grease inside your home.
  • Custom Functionality: Your kitchen, your way—grills, burners, storage, sinks, fridges, and more.

What’s Included in a Cramers Outdoor Kitchen

Every kitchen we build is custom-designed based on your preferences, space, and culinary needs. Typical features include:

Built to

Built-In Grills & Smokers

We install gas, propane, charcoal, or ceramic smokers depending on your taste and cooking style.

Side Burners & Cooktops

Prepare side dishes, sauces, or low-simmered meals alongside your main grill.

Countertops & Work Surfaces

Choose from granite, stone, concrete, or stainless steel for durable, beautiful prep areas.

Outdoor Sinks

Make cleanup easier with a built-in sink (cold water standard; hot water available with hookups).

Refrigeration

Undercounter fridges, wine coolers, or ice bins help keep drinks and ingredients ready to serve.

Cabinetry & Storage

We install weatherproof drawers and cabinets for utensils, cleaning supplies, or propane tanks.

Custom Layouts

L-shaped, U-shaped, island-style, or built against a retaining wall—your kitchen is designed to fit seamlessly into your backyard layout.

Optional Add-Ons

  • Pizza ovens
  • Kegerators
  • Warming drawers
  • Bar counters
  • Fire pits or fireplaces nearby
  • Integrated lighting and sound systems

Last in Charleston’s Coastal Climate

The Lowcountry’s humidity, salt air, and intense sun demand smart construction. We use:
    • Non-corrosive materials like stainless steel hardware and sealed stone counters
    • Moisture-resistant finishes to prevent mold or rot
    • UV-stable materials that won’t fade or warp over time
    • Proper drainage design so standing water never becomes an issue
Our installation team ensures every unit is safe, level, and connected to the proper utilities (with licensed partners as needed).

Seamless Integration Outdoor Space

Our outdoor kitchens aren’t built in isolation. We coordinate every design with your:

    • Existing hardscapes like patios, walkways, or retaining walls.
    • Surrounding landscaping, making sure nearby plants, trees, and beds remain undisturbed
    • Outdoor structures, including pergolas or pavilions for shade and shelter
    • Lighting and irrigation, ensuring no interference with your existing systems

We also make sure your kitchen complements the style and material palette of your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Request Yours

Do I need a permit for an outdoor kitchen in Charleston?

In many cases, yes. We’ll handle the permitting process as needed, including compliance with HOA guidelines or local codes.

How long does it take to build an outdoor kitchen?

Most projects take 3–5 weeks depending on complexity, permitting, and appliance availability.

Can I convert an existing grill area into a full kitchen?

Absolutely. We can expand or rebuild existing setups to include more features and better function.
