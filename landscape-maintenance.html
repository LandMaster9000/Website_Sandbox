Landscape Maintenance in Charleston, SC

A beautifully designed landscape is only as good as its ongoing care. At Cramers Landscaping, we provide comprehensive landscape maintenance services to keep your property healthy, polished, and seasonally refreshed—whether it’s a private residence, commercial property, or community development. Our team combines horticultural expertise with detailed site knowledge and proactive care plans tailored to Charleston’s climate. From routine upkeep to seasonal enhancements, we ensure your landscape continues to grow in value, curb appeal, and health throughout the year.

Why Landscape Maintenance Matters

Charleston’s coastal weather, mixed soil types, and seasonal storms require year-round attention to your outdoor space.
Without consistent upkeep, even the most beautiful design can fall into disrepair.

Our maintenance approach:

  • Extends the life of your plants and turf
  • Prevents overgrowth, weed infestation, and plant disease
  • Enhances property value and aesthetics
  • Supports long-term irrigation, lighting, and drainage function
  • Saves you money by preventing larger repairs or replanting

Whether you need weekly, monthly, or quarterly service, we tailor our plan to your property’s specific needs.

What’s Included in Our Landscape Maintenance Program

We offer complete solutions that go far beyond lawn mowing:

Bed Maintenance & Mulching

We remove weeds, edge beds, refresh mulch, and prune seasonal plantings for neat, defined spaces.

Shrub & Hedge Pruning

We trim and shape shrubs, privacy hedges, and foundation plantings based on natural growth cycles.

Seasonal Flower Rotations

Colorful annual installations for spring, summer, fall, and holidays to keep your landscape vibrant.

Tree & Palm Trimming

We remove dead branches and improve shape for safety, sunlight, and airflow.

Leaf Removal & Debris Clearing

Seasonal cleanup of leaves, pine straw, fallen limbs, and plant debris—especially after storms.

Turf Health Monitoring

We inspect for signs of disease, bare patches, pests, or drought stress and recommend solutions.

Irrigation Monitoring & Adjustments

We test and adjust sprinkler zones, schedules, and pressure for water efficiency and plant health.

Tailored to Property Type

We work with:

  • Residential homeowners
  • Apartment and condo complexes
  • HOAs and community associations
  • Commercial retail centers
  • Office parks and professional buildings
  • Vacation rental properties

Each maintenance plan is adapted to your goals, usage, and local landscape requirements.

Seasonal Maintenance Services

The Lowcountry’s climate presents unique seasonal challenges. Our seasonal care includes:

We also offer storm response services for emergency debris clearing and wind damage control.

Spring

Cutbacks, bed prep, fertilization, irrigation start-up, annual planting

Summer

Weekly monitoring, pest control, deadheading, mulch checks

Fall

Leaf removal, pruning, mulch replenishment, storm cleanup

Winter

Dormant cutbacks, plant protection, irrigation shutdown, hardscape inspections
