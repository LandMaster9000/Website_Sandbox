Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pool deck and why is it important?

A pool deck is the hard surface surrounding your swimming pool that provides space for walking, lounging, and entertaining. In Charleston’s humid climate, using durable, sealed concrete ensures safety and longevity while enhancing curb appeal and functionality.

How do you build a concrete pool deck?

Building a Charleston concrete pool deck involves site grading, installing forms, pouring reinforced concrete, and applying a finishing texture or sealant. Our team uses materials and methods suited for Charleston’s coastal conditions to prevent cracking and ensure lasting performance.

Can a damaged pool deck be resurfaced?

Yes. Charleston pool deck resurfacing can repair cracks, stains, and fading without a complete replacement. We use overlay systems that bond to the existing concrete, creating a smooth, renewed surface with slip-resistant options.

How often should a pool deck be sealed in Charleston?

Because of Charleston’s high humidity and salt air, we recommend sealing your pool deck every 2–3 years. As one of the top pool deck sealing companies near me, Cramers uses high-grade sealers that protect against moisture, UV damage, and chemical exposure.

How much does it cost to resurface or install a pool deck?

Costs vary depending on deck size, material finish, and condition. Since each property is unique, the best way to get accurate pricing is to schedule a free consultation with Cramers Landscaping - call 843-709-6140 to discuss your options.

Can I resurface my concrete pool deck myself?

While DIY resurfacing kits exist, professional resurfacing ensures long-term results. The process requires precise surface prep, mixing, and curing - especially in Charleston’s humid climate. Hiring a concrete pool deck company ensures your deck resists cracking, fading, and peeling.
