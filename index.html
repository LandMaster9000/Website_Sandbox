Charleston’s Premier Landscaping Company

Welcome to Cramers Landscaping, your trusted partner for expert landscape services in Charleston, South Carolina. We specialize in comprehensive landscaping solutions for residential and commercial properties, from new installations and hardscaping to seasonal maintenance and drainage systems. Our mission is simple: deliver high-quality outdoor environments that are both beautiful and built to last.

Whether you're looking to design your dream backyard, install a new irrigation system, or improve curb appeal with expert grading and enhancements, our team has the knowledge, equipment, and experience to make it happen. We take pride in delivering exceptional results that reflect the character and needs of each property we touch.

Expert Solutions Tailored Our Services

Cramers Landscaping offers a full range of landscape and hardscape services, tailored to meet the needs of homeowners, builders, HOAs, and commercial property managers.

Landscape Installation

Our team designs and installs lush, functional landscapes using native plantings, strategic bed layouts, and carefully chosen materials. We create timeless outdoor spaces that are both easy to maintain and seasonally vibrant.

Hardscape Installation

Why Homeowners and Builders Choose Cramers Landscaping

Deep Local Expertise

As a Charleston-based company, we understand the unique challenges and opportunities that come with Lowcountry landscaping. From coastal soil conditions and salt air exposure to heavy rainfall and drainage issues, our team knows how to design and install landscapes that thrive in this region.
We’ve completed projects in downtown Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Sullivans Island, West Ashley, Summerville, Folly Beach, and other surrounding communities—always customizing our approach to each environment and property style.

Quality-Driven Process

Our success is built on a streamlined, detail-oriented process that keeps clients informed and confident at every step:

We listen closely to your goals, budget, and timeline to understand the full scope of your project.

Our team proposes a thoughtful plan based on your property, lifestyle, and local conditions.

Using high-grade materials and proven techniques, we bring your outdoor vision to life.

We offer long-term landscape maintenance and system support to keep your investment thriving.
