Landscaping Summerville, SC

Cramers Landscaping is proud to serve Summerville, South Carolina with comprehensive landscaping services designed for growing families, established neighborhoods, and new construction homes. From landscape installation to ongoing maintenance and custom hardscapes, we create outdoor spaces that are as practical as they are beautiful. If you live in Summerville—or are moving into a new development in Dorchester or Berkeley County—our team is ready to bring your vision to life with locally trusted expertise, attention to detail, and top-tier materials built for Lowcountry living.

Landscaping for Life in Summerville

Known as the "Flower Town in the Pines," Summerville blends suburban charm with natural beauty. It’s one of the fastest-growing communities in the Charleston metro area, offering homeowners larger yards, tree-lined streets, and room to invest in outdoor living. At Cramers Landscaping, we help property owners make the most of their yards—whether it’s a complete backyard renovation, grading and drainage solutions, or a full sod and irrigation install. We also support HOAs and commercial property managers with large-scale installations and recurring maintenance contracts.

Full-Service Landscaping Solutions in Summerville

We offer a wide range of residential and commercial landscaping services to suit the needs of this growing community.
Landscape Installation

We install sod, native plantings, trees, and flower beds that work with Summerville’s soil conditions, shade patterns, and long growing season. From backyard retreats to front yard upgrades, we tailor each plan to your goals.

Hardscape Installation

Patios, retaining walls, and walkways enhance property functionality, support drainage, and create inviting outdoor spaces for family gatherings and entertaining.

Irrigation Systems Installation

Protect your investment with a properly installed irrigation system. We use smart, zoned layouts that conserve water while keeping your lawn and beds consistently healthy.

Landscape Maintenance

We offer regular mowing, trimming, edging, weeding, and irrigation checks—ideal for busy families or homeowners who want professional results year-round.

Mulching & Bed Maintenance

Maintain clean, well-defined beds with professional mulch installation using pine straw, hardwood mulch, or decorative stone.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

We shape and maintain your shrubs, hedges, and ornamental trees to enhance curb appeal and support long-term health.

Drainage & Grading

Combat standing water and erosion with proper grading, French drains, and dry creek beds. Our team designs with both drainage and stormwater compliance in mind.

Site Structures

Install pergolas, fencing, or patio features that turn your backyard into a true extension of your home.
