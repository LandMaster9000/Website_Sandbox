Landscaping Services in Johns Island, SC

Home / Johns Island, SC

Cramers Landscaping is proud to serve Johns Island, SC with professional landscaping services built on craftsmanship, local knowledge, and long-term value. Whether you're in Stonoview, Kiawah River, Brownswood, or anywhere across Johns Island, our team is ready to transform your outdoor space.

From landscape design and installation to hardscaping, drainage, and maintenance, we offer full-service solutions that reflect Johns Island's natural Lowcountry character and your personal vision.

Local Expertise for Johns Island Landscapes

Johns Island is one of the most rapidly growing areas in the Charleston region, with a mix of new residential communities, rural estates, and preserved natural landscapes. That growth brings unique landscaping opportunities — and challenges. At Cramers Landscaping, we understand the soils, drainage patterns, and community character that define Johns Island.

We know how to design for:

  • Humid summers and heavy Lowcountry rainfall
  • Clay and mixed soils common across Johns Island
  • Drainage challenges on flat and low-lying lots
  • Salt-tolerant plantings near tidal areas
  • HOA and community design guidelines in new developments

Our work is tailored not just to your vision — but to Johns Island's unique landscape environment and character.

Full-Service Landscaping on Johns Island

Whether you own a primary home, new construction property, or rural estate, we provide everything needed to elevate your outdoor space.

Landscape Installation

We install sod, native plants, trees, mulch beds, and more. Our landscape installs are designed with Johns Island's climate and soil conditions in mind, built for longevity and seasonal performance.

Hardscape Installation

Build a custom patio, retaining wall, or walkway that complements your home's layout and improves outdoor living. We use materials that pair beautifully with Johns Island's natural and architectural character.

Irrigation Systems Installation

Keep your landscape healthy year-round with a zoned irrigation system, smart controller, and proper drainage layout. We also offer ongoing irrigation maintenance and seasonal adjustments.

Landscape Maintenance

Our crew handles routine mowing, trimming, edging, seasonal cleanup, and more so your Johns Island property always looks its best — whether it's a new build or an established estate.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Maintain tree health and shape, improve safety, and boost curb appeal with professional pruning and shaping services. Johns Island's mature tree canopy is one of its defining features, and we help you protect and enhance it.

    Drainage & Grading

    Flat terrain and heavy rainfall make drainage a common challenge across Johns Island. We solve pooling, erosion, and runoff issues with targeted grading, French drains, and catch basin installations.
