Backyard and Pool Pavilion Projects in Charleston, SC

A pavilion is the difference between a yard you look at and a yard you use. Unlike a pergola, a pavilion has a solid roof, so the space underneath works through an August afternoon and through a Lowcountry thunderstorm. That is usually what people are describing when they say they want shade. We build freestanding backyard pavilions, poolside pavilions tied into an existing deck or patio, and full outdoor living pavilions with a kitchen, fireplace and lighting built in, across Charleston, West Ashley, James Island, Daniel Island and Mount Pleasant.

Pavilion Builds for Lowcountry Homes

Poolside is where a pavilion earns its keep. A pool deck with nowhere to get out of the sun gets used an hour at a time. Put a covered structure beside it and it becomes somewhere people stay. We build pool pavilions around the existing deck and can tie in an outdoor kitchen or outdoor lighting. Size and placement matter more than most people expect. A pavilion that is too small reads as an afterthought, and one that is too large crowds the yard and cuts light from the house. We work the footprint out on site, against the pool or patio it serves, the sightlines from indoors, and where the afternoon sun actually falls.
