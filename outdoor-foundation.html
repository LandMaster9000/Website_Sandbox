Expert Concrete and Structural Base Work for Lasting Outdoor Living

What Are Outdoor Foundations?

Outdoor foundations refer to the structural support systems beneath outdoor living features such as kitchens, pavilions, pergolas, sheds, and patios.

Depending on the use case and structure type, these can include:

  • Concrete slabs or pads
  • Footings or piers
  • Block and mortar foundations
  • Paver bases or compacted aggregate
  • Floating decks or platforms

Each type of foundation serves a specific purpose. Our team recommends the ideal option
based on the size, weight, and design of your outdoor structure—and the unique soil and
grading conditions of your property.

Why Foundations Matter in Outdoor Projects in Outdoor Projects

A solid foundation ensures:

  • Structural Stability: Prevents tilting, cracking, or sinking over time.
  • Safety and Compliance: Meets load-bearing requirements and local building codes.
  • Water Management: Properly directs runoff and avoids water pooling near structures.
  • Longevity: Extends the life of your outdoor investment.
  • Integrated Design: Coordinates with hardscapes, irrigation, and landscape layouts.

Whether we’re anchoring a pergola or preparing a slab for a full kitchen, we plan with durability and future compatibility in mind.

Types of Outdoor Foundations We Build

Concrete Slabs

Ideal for outdoor kitchens, hot tubs, seating areas, and heavy structures.
  • Poured to custom dimensions
  • Wire mesh or rebar reinforcement
  • Thickness typically 4–6 inches
  • Control joints to prevent cracking
Available finishes: smooth, broomed, stamped

Footings & Piers

Used for vertical load-bearing features like pergolas, porches, and pavilions.
  • Dug below frost line
  • Sized based on beam span and structure weight
  • Anchored brackets or post bases installed after curing

Concrete Block Foundations

Used for elevated outdoor kitchens or as bases for walls and planter structures.

  • Mortar-set blocks with optional rebar
  • Skim-coated or veneered to match hardscape

Gravel & Paver Bases

For patios, seating areas, and walkways that don’t require a solid slab.

  • Layered compacted gravel
  • Optional paver sand and geotextile fabric
  • Edge restraint systems included

Custom Platforms or Decks

Floating foundations for wood-based features on uneven or moisture-prone lots.

  • Pressure-treated framing
  • Ground contact-rated footings or blocks
  • Composite or hardwood decking options

Our Outdoor Foundation Installation Process

Site Evaluation & Planning

We assess your property’s slope, soil condition, and drainage needs.

Design & Engineering

Custom layout and measurements for slab, footing, or pier spacing. Code-compliant sizing.

Excavation & Grading

We level the site and dig to necessary depths, removing any debris or roots.

Formwork & Reinforcement

Concrete forms are built and steel reinforcement is added for strength.

Pouring & Finishing

Concrete is poured, screeded, and finished based on your design (smooth, textured, etc.).

Curing & Integration

We allow proper cure time, then integrate irrigation, lighting, or structural anchors as needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need permits for a concrete foundation?

Yes, in most cases. Cramers Landscaping will handle any necessary permitting and inspections for your project.

Can you install a new structure on an existing patio or foundation?

Sometimes, but we’ll assess whether your current slab or surface meets code and load requirements.

How long does it take to install a foundation?

Typical slab or footing projects take 3–5 days including excavation, pouring, and curing. Larger or complex projects may take longer.
